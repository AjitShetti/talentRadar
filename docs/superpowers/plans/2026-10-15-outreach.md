# Outreach Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** An outreach-first job search. Per-user company threads hold the people in them, every touch and a per-person follow-up clock. Deterministic suggestions say whom to reach. Drafts are budgeted, with a template fallback. Good-first-issues come from GitHub. Due follow-ups and one suggestion feed the Overview day plan. Nothing is ever sent for the user.

**Architecture:**
- Pure rules live in `domain/`:
  - `outreach.py`: the clock, stages, due items, bounded text, IST days.
  - `outreach_rank.py`: company and people ranking.
  - `outreach_drafts.py`: formats, limits, templates.
- Thin I/O lives in `services/outreach/`: threads, people, touches, suggestions, drafts, OSS and briefing cards.
- One router, `api/routers/outreach.py`, plus one cron endpoint in `api/routers/ingest.py`.
- Frontend: the `/outreach` page with a thread drawer, a block in the company drawer, a nav item, and two new Overview cards ranked in `buildActions`.

**Tech Stack:**
- Backend: FastAPI, async SQLAlchemy, Alembic (raw SQL, IF NOT EXISTS), httpx, Groq via `services.llm._chat`, `CacheBackend`.
- Frontend: Next.js 14 App Router and lucide-react (`Handshake`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-outreach-design.md`. The design system is in `DESIGN.md`.

## Global Constraints

- **Nothing is sent for the user.** No endpoint sends a message; the UI has no "Send" verb. Channel actions only copy text and open a URL in a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
- **LinkedIn, X and email are never fetched.** LinkedIn links come from stored URLs or `domain.linkedin_links.person_search_link(name, company)`. Email appears only when it is a stored `company_contacts.email` that is user-saved (`user_id` set) or has a `source_url`, or a name-only person's typed email.
- **No LLM on any read or ranking path.** Only `POST /outreach/threads/{id}/draft` calls the model, through `services.llm._chat` with `model=get_settings().groq_fast_model, temperature=0.5, max_tokens=1200, reasoning_effort="low"`.
- **Budgets:**
  - `PER_USER_DAILY_DRAFTS = 20`. The key is `tr:budget:drafts:user:<user_id>:<IST date>`, and the referral ask shares it.
  - `DAILY_OUTREACH_DRAFT_BUDGET = 150`. The key is `tr:budget:llm:outreach:<UTC date>`.
  - Both are claimed with `CacheBackend.incr` (48 h TTL): increment first, fail open.
- **Clock constants** (`domain/outreach.py`):
  - follow-ups: `FOLLOW_UP_AFTER_DAYS = (5, 7)`, `MAX_FOLLOW_UPS = 2`
  - cooling: `COLD_AFTER_DAYS = 10`, `RECONTACT_AFTER_DAYS = 90`
  - date limits: `SNOOZE_MAX_DAYS = 14`, `BACKDATE_MAX_DAYS = 30`
  - pacing: `SAME_COMPANY_WEEK_SOFT_LIMIT = 2`
- **Caps:**
  - counts: `MAX_OPEN_THREADS = 100` (409), `MAX_PEOPLE_PER_THREAD = 12` (409), `MAX_TOUCHES_PER_THREAD = 60` (409)
  - text: `NOTE_MAX = 500`, `NEXT_STEP_MAX = 140`, `BODY_MAX = 2000` (422)
  - daily target: `DEFAULT_DAILY_TARGET = 3`, `MAX_DAILY_TARGET = 10`
  - retention: `THREAD_RETENTION_DAYS = 365`
- **"Today" is IST**: a fixed `timezone(timedelta(hours=5, minutes=30))`. Never `zoneinfo`.
- **Privacy:**
  - Every service function takes `user_id` and filters on it. Another user's thread, person or touch is a **404**, never a 403.
  - Logs carry ids and counts only, never names, notes or drafts.
  - Reply notes, next steps and other people's details never reach the model.
- **Prompt text** is flattened and bounded with `agents.interview.role_context._flat` and fenced: `<<<COMPANY>>>`, `<<<POSTING>>>`, `<<<ISSUE>>>`, `<<<EARLIER MESSAGE>>>`, `<<<USER NOTE>>>`.
- **GitHub:**
  - Only the scheduled step calls GitHub; a page load never does.
  - Intervals: 2.2 s apart (≤ 60 a run) with `GITHUB_TOKEN`, 6.5 s apart (≤ 20) without.
  - A 403 or 429 stops the run.
- **Migration** `storage/migrations/versions/017_outreach.py`:
  - `revision = "017_outreach"`, `down_revision = "016_company_is_tech"`.
  - It creates five tables and alters none.
- **Code quality:**
  - Routers stay thin; mypy is strict; ruff runs with the isort first-party list.
  - Every scheduled step and every briefing producer never raises.
- **Frontend:**
  - `frontend/lib/api.ts` is the only API client.
  - Amber is spent only on due lamps, the one primary button per surface, and focus.
  - Everything is keyboard operable and works at 375 px. No hover-only affordance survives below 920 px.
  - No streak, no "messages sent" metric.
- **Sample and test data** use fictional people and companies, never a real person attached to a real company.

## Review Focus

1. **A thread or person id belonging to another user.** It must be a 404 on every route, read and write, with no row touched. Test: `test_other_users_thread_is_404_everywhere` in Task 13.
2. **"I sent it" clicked twice, or after a reply.** The second click must not count as a second first-message or move a replied person back to waiting. Tests: `test_double_first_out_is_a_follow_up_not_a_second_reach` in Task 8 and `test_out_after_reply_changes_nothing` in Task 1.
3. **Day edges in India.** A touch at 23:50 IST counts for that IST day, not the UTC one. A follow-up due "today" is due from 00:00 IST. Tests: `test_reached_today_uses_ist_day` in Task 8 and `test_ist_day_boundary` in Task 1.
4. **A model reply that ignores the length or adds a subject to a LinkedIn note.** It must be cut to the format's limit, and only email keeps a subject. Tests: `test_draft_is_cut_to_the_format_limit` and `test_subject_only_for_email` in Task 12.
5. **A GitHub search item pointing outside the org, or with junk labels.** It must be dropped or trimmed, never linked. Test: `test_parse_search_drops_foreign_urls_and_trims_labels` in Task 10.

## Payload shapes (shared by services, router and `api.ts`)

Timestamps are ISO-8601 UTC strings; `*_at` dates for next steps are `YYYY-MM-DD`.

- **Channel** `{channel: "linkedin"|"email"|"x"|"github", url, label, direct: bool, source_host: str|None}`
  - LinkedIn: a stored profile → `label "LinkedIn"`, `direct True`. Otherwise the search link → `label "Search LinkedIn"`, `direct False`.
  - Email: `url "mailto:<addr>"`, `label <addr>`. `source_host` is the host of `source_url`, or `None` for user-saved.
- **Person** `{id, contact_id|None, name, title|None, kind, state: "planned"|"waiting"|"replied"|"cold"|"dropped", verified, user_saved, source_url|None, source_host|None, channels: Channel[], first_sent_at|None, last_outbound_at|None, follow_ups_sent, next_follow_up_at|None, replied_at|None, draft: "first"|"follow_up"|None}`
- **SuggestedPerson** `{contact_id, name, title|None, kind, verified, user_saved, source_host|None, reason, score}`
- **Touch** `{id, person_id|None, person_name|None, channel, direction: "out"|"in", kind: "first"|"follow_up"|"reply"|"other", note|None, body|None, happened_at}`
- **IssueSnapshot** `{org, status, total_count, issues: [{repo, number, title, url, labels: str[], updated_at, comments}], fetched_at}`
- **ThreadSummary** `{id, company_id|None, company_name, company_summary|None, stage: "planned"|"waiting"|"replied"|"cold"|"closed", outcome|None, people_count, lead: {name, state}|None, due: bool, next_due_at|None, last_touch_at|None, job: {id|None, title}|None, application: {id, status}|None, source, created_at}`
- **ThreadDetail** is ThreadSummary plus `{note|None, next_step|None, next_step_at|None, closed_at|None, company: {stage, sector, area, last_funding_at, linkedin_url}|None, people: Person[], suggested_people: SuggestedPerson[], touches: Touch[], issues: IssueSnapshot|None, careers_url|None, linkedin_links: [{label, url}], pacing_note|None}`
- **DueItem** `{thread_id, person_id|None, kind: "follow_up"|"next_step", due_at, company_name, person_name|None, follow_up_number|None, last_outbound_at|None, next_step|None}`
- **Home** `{target: {daily, reached_today}, conversations_started, due: DueItem[], threads: ThreadSummary[]}`
- **Suggestion** `{company_id, name, summary|None, area|None, stage|None, sector|None, score, reasons: str[], top_person: {name, title|None, kind}|None}`. **Suggestions** `{suggestions: Suggestion[], profile_complete: bool}`.
- **Draft** `{format, message, subject|None, generated: bool, reason: None|"daily_cap"|"budget"|"model_unavailable", remaining_today, max_chars}`
- **Create** `{thread: ThreadSummary, created: bool}`. **CompanyThread** `{thread: ThreadSummary|None}`. **Settings** `{daily_target}`.
