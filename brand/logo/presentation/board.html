What we heard

The brief

A job-intelligence app for students and early-career engineers in India: it finds roles worth applying to, tells you whether a posting is still live, and gets you ready for the interview. The mark has to feel sharp, honest and calm, work at 16 px in a browser tab, and sit in a true-black, Apple-style interface.

sharphonestcalmalert

Success: reads as a radar without the clichés (concentric rings, crosshairs, RSS-like arcs), survives 16 px, works in one colour, and sits naturally on black.

Final design

Sweep

A radar quadrant mid-sweep: the beam cut in as one line, and a blip it has found.

  • One solid shape, so it holds up as a favicon and app icon
  • The beam and blip are negative space: the signal found in the noise
  • System blue #0A84FF, the app's own accent, on true black or white
  • A dedicated small cut keeps beam and blip apart at 16 px
